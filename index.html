THE VISION

A generation that learns space by exploring it.

SNIDF is the space edition of NIDF — Navigate, Learn & Challenge Your Future — an immersive STEAM platform. Instead of memorizing facts, learners walk the surface of Mars, fly through the Milky Way and run experiments in orbit. An AI tutor watches how each student reasons and builds a curriculum that always meets them exactly at their edge.

Built for the NASA challenge — a working preview of the full AR + AI platform.

🥽 Immersive by default

Augmented and virtual reality replace the textbook. You don't read about a black hole — you stand next to one.

🧠 An AI that adapts

A personalized curriculum and real-time difficulty tuning. Every student gets their own path and pace.

🎮 Learning as a game

Missions, XP, levels and badges turn curiosity into momentum that keeps students coming back.

🔭 NASA-grade science

Real data and real phenomena, retold from the inside — the topics NASA wants the world to understand.
